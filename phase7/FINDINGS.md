# Self-check access does not certify complete work

Published locally 28 September 2026 (29 September UTC). The fixed-reader
source-native experiment is complete. The inherited energy selects useful compact
evidence on one new task, but **does not beat ordinary dependency access over the
two-task sequence**. Ordinary empirical reuse supplies the best unassisted artifact
coverage of the explicit requirements, at substantially higher work. Generated
self-tests can miss the actual bug, assert incorrect behavior, or cause repeat loops.
An executable grader can also miss a requested deliverable.

Investigator: observed gpt-6-astra/high, OpenAI Codex CLI 0.158.0, session
`01a0e7e4-68e2-7fb2-9a5d-2ea6873b86b8`. No independent reviewer or subagent ran.
Root scientific review remains separate. Starting context includes phase 6 and its
exposed failures. [Protocol](PROTOCOL.md), [sources](SOURCES.md), [runtime](runtime.json)
and [reproduction](REPRODUCE.md) specify exact assets, scope and provenance.

## Stopping reason and next bounded step

Close on explanatory progress, not an energy win: new source-native behavior works,
earlier learned preference transfers without refitting, ordinary alternatives remain
strong, and both self-test and examiner limits are concretely isolated. The fixed
reader sometimes produces useful checks, sometimes wrong expectations, and sometimes
repeats them. A scope reminder repairs a real omitted requirement at measured cost.

Before adding capacity to the energy, the next bounded study should freeze a shared
public-requirement completion checklist and a rule for handling repeated/incorrect
own checks, develop these on the now-exposed cases, then use new edits. Include an
ordinary inventory-first access policy if it can avoid redundant source delivery.
Keep feedback provenance explicit: own assertions, executed checks and independently
reviewed requirements are different label sources. Do not train on own-check pass
as though this phase established it as correctness. No broad runtime redevelopment,
foundation training, sibling-study edit or retirement of the broader question follows.
