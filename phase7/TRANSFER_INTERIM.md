final artifact. The code correctly snapshots outer replacement rules, one-shot
inner pairs and stopwords, preserves None/empty options and leaves caller lists
unchanged in the checked cases. Generated patches update the requested docstring.

Ordinary access generates one wrong assertion expecting capital letters under
`lowercase=True`, then corrects its check without changing the working code.
Empirical reuse repeatedly submits the same invalid nested-pair fixture six times.
Its production artifact is test-complete, but it exhausts the budget without an
explicit finish. Broad and energy-selected contexts lead to respectively nine
and eight passing own checks, without this repair loop. Passing own tests is not
an examiner certificate; development already demonstrated the opposite cases.

The energy's lower work on this task is measured after actual reads, checking and
repair, not inferred from prompt length. Its initial evidence is 4,873 bytes,
ordinary's 6,180; both also read source. Most of the 10,397-token gap to ordinary
involves differing subsequent verification behavior. One seeded trajectory per
delivery cannot establish a robust causal account of that difference or a general
cost advantage. The frozen energy still just negates a set score and enumerates
ordinary proposals; there is no energy-optimizer effect here.

The shared accepted revision comes from the first passing ordinary attempt, not
from the learned arm. The second task begins from that same revision for every
policy. Its frozen energy chooses `all`, contrasting with `root_only` here. Final
paired results, maintenance, aggregate costs and independent native replay follow
after the second task closes; no later feedback changes the weights or interface.
