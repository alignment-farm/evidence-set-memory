# Developed claim frozen before fresh generation

28 September 2026. The development comparison used four authored histories,
20 requests, and no LLM. A deterministic public quote tool performs complete
work and produces outcome feedback; the primary-model reader remains separate.

Initial random exploration yielded 4 successes among 320 attempts. An ordinary
closure-plus-perturbation exploration recipe yielded 24/760. Eight-parameter
pointwise-in-set logistic classification trained for 1,000 steps reached 12/20
complete development tasks, failing value correction and reference revision.
Increasing steps to 10,000 reduced loss but retained 12/20. The learned weight
on current fraction was negative in both classification fits. This is observed
misranking, not a claim that the objective can never learn authority.

Within-task pairwise logistic fitting to differences between the SAME successful
and unsuccessful attempts reaches 20/20 under exhaustive three-record search.
This intervention changes objective and effective weighting; it does not isolate
which of those changes explains the improvement. It establishes a functioning
small learner in the authored regime without new labels or a neural architecture.
Pointwise-only features remain at 4/20, all from legitimate cached repair outputs;
their raw selected sets are never sufficient. That is an ablation, not the
competent ordinary baseline.

Ordinary current-revision dependency closure also reaches 20/20 without learned
scoring. One-swap minimization of the same learned negative score reaches 16/20;
all four failures are after changed references. Public stale/missing checks and
ordinary fallback repair every arm. Score sign has no scientific effect; the
search path can have one. These comparisons favor direct source access here.

## Fresh test, fixed now

Generate six new histories (seed 28102026) only after the freeze record, with
new identities and values. Evaluate the five declared stages under ordinary,
exact pairwise score, one-swap energy, zero and pointwise-ablation arms. Check:

1. Does the acquired exact scorer keep complete outcomes across new identities,
   changed values, changed references and preservation requests?
2. Does the swap search lose on changed references, even with the same energy?
3. Does ordinary closure match quality with less measured selection work?

No inferential population claim: six constructed histories are the clustered
units, not thirty independent natural tasks. They share the development generator
and representation. Passing is narrow parameter/identity transfer, not novel
relation transfer. No confirmation update is allowed. Past output caching is
allowed for all arms and validates current lineage; confirmations can create
working artifacts but cannot update weights or inform claim selection.

If serving is available, use a fixed Qwen3 8B reader on the first fresh history,
with ordinary/exact/energy selected contexts and diagnostic full-current,
one-record-removed and stale contexts, capped at 24 unique requests. Deduplicate
identical request/context pairs and preserve that reuse map. Evaluate exact
complete JSON outputs, separately from deterministic completion. Stale validity
is checked by the controller against public archive metadata; primary success
requires current citations. No tuning after these reader results.

The expected phase endpoint is an explained direct-addressing regime, not an
EBM advantage. Broader usefulness, unstructured discovery and LLM-mediated
acquisition remain untested even if all fresh executable outcomes pass.
