## Investigator and evidence policy

Use `gpt-6-astra` for investigators and reviewers until the user changes the
22 September policy. Record actual reasoning settings, provider/harness versions,
starting context, interventions and known costs. Mark unknown or user-reported
provenance explicitly. Participant, specialist and teacher models remain choices
within the study; the investigator policy does not prescribe them.

Specify what survives between episodes: source records, representations, learned
weights, caches and working artifacts. Separate inference-time optimization of
a candidate set from an update to persistent model parameters. A set scorer and
its negative energy can implement exactly the same ordering; identify a concrete
method difference before claiming an EBM effect.

Use complete downstream outcomes with competent alternatives, including an
ordinary policy that can assemble sets. Keep source information, ordinary reuse
and repair available. Record candidate discovery separately from selection.
Shorter prompts or better retrieval metrics alone do not establish usefulness.
Declared authority and source corrections remain explicit; a learned low energy
does not certify truth, scope or permission.

Workload discovery and motivated construction are legitimate. Public assets are
leads until their actual data, labels and evaluators are checked. Frozen QA cases
are not continuing experience merely because they are ordered. Keep benchmark
supervision, authored teaching and learner-earned feedback distinct. Use only
eligible prior evidence for acquisition, then fresh later material for claims
developed through diagnosis. Preserve unsuccessful runs and source revisions.

When acquisition fails, continue bounded diagnosis that separates plausible
causes or establishes a functioning regime. Finite gradients are insufficient.
Close a phase on explanatory progress, a demonstrated limit or a concrete resource
constraint, rather than an early null or a requirement that neural learning wins.
A publication does not automatically retire the broader research question.
