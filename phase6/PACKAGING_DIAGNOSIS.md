# Whole-file packaging diagnosis

Declared during initial development, after both ordinary and all-source chunked
arms completed the first edit. Both reread `main.py` and `parser.py` before patching,
despite their text already being available in AST records in the all-source arm.
This is evidence of a possible delivery/interface issue, not missing candidates.

After finishing the existing run unchanged, repeat the same two development
requirements and source revisions with (a) ordinary dependency-selected whole files,
and (b) all production files intact in source order. Keep reader, task, tests, scope,
call cap and public repair unchanged. Preserve first runs. These runs may improve
the ordinary baseline and reveal that optimizing chunks was solving a packaging
artifact. Their outcomes remain development data, never fresh confirmation.

Before fresh claims, include the competent file-based alternatives in selection.
A cost learner, if developed, must account for subsequent source reads rather
than optimizing initial evidence bytes alone. Invariant complete feedback would
identify a cost preference at most, not learned sufficiency or new repair ability.
